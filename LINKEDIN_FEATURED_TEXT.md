**Project name**  
Unicorn Market Intelligence | SQL, Python & Power BI

**Project description**  
Built an end-to-end analytics case study examining 1,074 companies in a historical unicorn dataset, focusing on the 732 that first achieved unicorn status in 2019–2021.

- Used PostgreSQL joins, CTEs and ranking logic to identify the three industries with the highest combined number of newly recorded unicorns.
- Used Python and Pandas to reproduce and validate industry cohorts, headquarters-country breakdowns and quality checks.
- Created a polished, interactive data visualization and an editable one-page Power BI project with DAX and Power Query.
- Documented methodology and historical-data limitations to distinguish unicorn formation from snapshot valuations and investment returns.

**A finding worth highlighting:** 400 of the 732 new unicorns (54.6%) recorded between 2019 and 2021 came from fintech, internet software and e-commerce in this historical dataset.
